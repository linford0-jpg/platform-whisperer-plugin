# Platform Whisperer

Platform Whisperer tells you which platform your London train is likely to leave from, before the station board shows it. It covers London Euston and London Paddington, and Claude can ask it for the next departures, look up one train by destination, time or headcode, and report how accurate its predictions have been.

## Use it

Install the plugin, connect Platform Whisperer from the plugin's Connectors tab, and sign in with Google. Then ask things like "Which platform is the next Manchester train from Euston?", "Is the 1H16 running late?" or "How accurate are your predictions?" The included skill makes Claude say whether a platform has been released or is only a prediction, and always remind you to check the station screens.

## Data

The connector is read only. It sends the station, train or time you ask about to platformwhisperer.com and returns departures and predictions. It shares nothing about you beyond your sign-in email, which is held only to run your account. Predictions are unofficial and may be wrong. Privacy notice: https://platformwhisperer.com/privacy.
